Who it is for

TechBridge is an initiative of Baselink Services Limited. Its purpose is to connect what people learn with the practical work they are expected to do.

Programs are open to prospective interns and learners who want practical experience in data analytics or web development.

  • You learn by doing. The internship is practical and project-based.
  • Two clear tracks. Data Analytics or Web Development.
  • A short commitment. The internship lasts 30 days.
  • Recognition for good work. A recommendation letter is possible based on successful participation and performance.
  • A community. Learners connect in the TechBridge WhatsApp Community.
